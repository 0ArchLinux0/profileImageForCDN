# Grey Visual — Brand & optional monetization roadmap (2026-10-26)

**Core identity:** Grey Visual | **Guess first. See why.** Independent faceless English micro-visuals that turn an intuitive prediction into a mathematically honest graphic demonstration. Not generic scraped trivia, pseudo-scientific shocking facts, or a hard-sell account.

## Editorial pillars

1. **Chance** — birthday paradox, collisions, repeated independent probabilities, counterintuitive lotteries with honest assumptions.
2. **Shape** — equal perimeter/different area, geometric transformations, misleading visual intuitions, explanatory geometry.
3. **Scale** — orders of magnitude, compounding, algorithms and meaningful physical comparison.

Every piece: **GUESS → SEE → REVEAL → WHY**. One question, one visualization, one relevant equation or qualification. Originality should derive from the *visual proof*, not attention hacking. Keep design consistent while allowing topic-specific scene code.

**Four-post pilot by 2026-10-26:** GV-001 scale of 1M/1B seconds (live), GV-003 1% repeated probability (live), GV-004 geometry (preview planned), GV-002 birthday pair probability (preview planned). Use **geometry before birthday** to avoid two 50%-crossing probability stories back-to-back.

## Stage gates (hypotheses, not promises)

### Stage 0 — now through 4 weeks
Goal: 4–8 *original* Reels at sustainable 1–2/week with <=15 min owner review/post where practical (measure actual), preserve original source and 24h/72h/7d age-normalized Insight. No paid funnel. More production only after owner approves current two.

### Stage 1 — 1–3 months
Goal: 12–24 reusable, high-quality posts at a feasible cadence and at least **two** topics that attract nonfollowers, saves, sends and decent completion across multiple posts; do not call statistical significance on tiny samples. Build a reusable **Visual Explainer Library**: probability curve, geometry morph, time scale, label placement, cover exporter, source-backed test fixtures.

### Stage 2 — 3–6 months (conditional on audience)
Publish a lightweight free **interactive Visual Puzzle Library** on existing website/GitHub Pages rather than create a new paid SaaS. Each Reel maps to an interactive explanation with sliders (birthday group size, w of fixed perimeter, number of repeated trials). Host as open or free, traffic optional. Make no claim about existing account link/follower growth until tested.

### Stage 3 — 6–12 months (optional)
Validate one channel at a time when relevant inbound demand appears:
1. Original interactive puzzle packs / teacher-friendly diagrams and exercise sheets.
2. Sponsorship / licensing from aligned education/math/design/developer tools with transparent paid partnership labeling.
3. Longer YouTube math visual explanations as a separate, clearly attributed repurposing strategy where a topic warrants depth.
4. Platform-native subscriptions/gifts only if the account is actually eligible and followers request them; avoid assumed payout eligibility or tax treatment.

Avoid compulsory monetization. **Success metric** can simply be a growing reusable math/art codebase and people who like the account. No paywall before there is a real reason to pay.

## Recommended technical evolution (incremental)

- `facts/<id>.json`: precise claim, source and assumptions, deterministic tests.
- `visuals/<id>.py`: original data-driven transition with invariant asserts.
- `storyboard/<id>.json`: beat timings, screen-safe bounds and typography.
- `release/<id>/`: video/cover/caption and immutable source manifest with checksums.
- Regression QA: render/ffprobe/full decode; random representative frames and time-indexed screenshots; 360px mobile look; version fonts; creative criteria separate from technical PASS.
- Observe real errors and fix targeted common modules instead of building an over-engineered dashboard/AI orchestrator. Keep rendering offline, private credentials out of Git.
- In future, A/B tests can experiment with same source/story and one varied element; do **not** claim a causal win by comparing two completely different videos.
